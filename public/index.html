Welcome to VibeOffice.work.

A free office suite that runs in your browser: a word processor, a spreadsheet and a presentation editor for .docx, .xlsx and .pptx files.

  • QuireDocuments with track changes, comments, footnotes, tables of contents and mail merge. Opens and saves .docx, .rtf, Markdown, HTML and text; prints to PDF.
  • LedgerSpreadsheets with 519 functions, charts, PivotTable reports and conditional formatting. Opens and saves .xlsx, .csv, XML Spreadsheet 2003 and HTML; prints to PDF.
  • LecternPresentations with transitions, animations, charts and IntelliArt diagrams. Opens and saves .pptx; exports PDF, PNG and HTML.
  • Your files stay whole. What you don't change is saved as it was, even what the apps can't edit yet, so a file goes back to whoever sent it intact.
  • Private by design. Everything runs on this computer: nothing is uploaded, and there is no account to make.
  • Nothing new to learn. Classic menus and toolbars, or a ribbon: choose one under Themes.
  • Works offline. After the first visit it opens without a connection, and Chrome and Edge can install it as an app.
  • In your language. Menus and dialogs in the languages below (Settings), and new documents with each language's fonts and page setup.

About VibeOffice.work

VibeOffice.work: Quire (documents), Ledger (spreadsheets) and Lectern (presentations).

Everything runs in your browser; documents are opened and saved on your own computer. The Recent Files list keeps a copy of each file in this browser so it can be reopened; Clear Recent Documents removes them.
