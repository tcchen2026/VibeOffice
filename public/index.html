VibeOffice

A free office suite that runs in your browser. Nothing to install, no account, and your files never leave your computer.

Quire

Word processor

Write letters, reports and theses with styles, tables, footnotes and tracked changes. Opens and saves .docx, RTF and web pages. Spelling and a thesaurus work offline.

Ledger

Spreadsheet

Over 500 functions, charts, sorting, filters and pivot summaries. Opens and saves .xlsx and CSV, password-protected workbooks included. Prints to PDF.

Lectern

Presentations

Build slides with design templates, shapes, charts and tables. Present full screen with transitions and animations. Opens and saves .pptx.
