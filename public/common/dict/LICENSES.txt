Offline proofing word lists and thesaurus (Quire, Ledger)
==========================================================

en_US.words and en_GB.words are expanded from the Hunspell English dictionaries distributed with
LibreOffice (https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries, en/), which are derived from SCOWL.
Word commonness levels come from the English Speller Database (SCOWL, https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist).

en.thes is a trimmed copy of th_en_US_v2.dat from the same LibreOffice package, derived from WordNet.

The 10 level includes the 1000 most common English words (according to
the Moby (TM) Words II [MWords] package), a subset of the 1000 most
common words on the Internet (again, according to Moby Words II), and
frequently class 16 from Brian Kelk's "UK English Wordlist
with Frequency Classification".
